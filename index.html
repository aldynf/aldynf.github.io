About

I'm the person behind the scene — designing the system, shaping the story, and making sure every piece lands.

With a background in broadcast design, motion graphics, and public-sector communication, I've spent over a decade learning how to make complex ideas feel simple. Not dumbed down — just clear.

I've worked across government agencies, tech startups, media brands, and diplomatic accounts. Each taught me something different about how people receive information — and how to meet them where they are.

My work lives at the intersection of strategy and craft: I think in systems, but I care about every detail — down to the sound mix on a 60-second reel.
